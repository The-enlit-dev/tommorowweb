## The brief

Vishwa Sports Academy (VSA) teaches karate in Pimpri-Chinchwad and Pune. Its website lived on Wix. The goal was a site that feels as disciplined as the training itself, gives parents and students the practical information they look for, and makes enquiring about classes straightforward.

## What we built

- A bold, high-contrast visual identity drawn from karate itself: the red circle, the kanji 空手道 and strong, condensed type.
- Coach profiles, class details, tournament and belt-exam listings, and a blog.
- A photo gallery with an accessible lightbox that works with a keyboard and screen readers.
- A registration and enquiry form powered by Netlify Forms, so enquiries arrive without a paid form service.

## How it’s built

The site was rebuilt as a static Astro site, deployed on Netlify. Static pages load quickly on mobile connections and can be hosted on Netlify’s free tier — a lighter, cheaper setup than a page-builder subscription.

## Where it stands

The site is live as a preview. Details marked *to confirm* and placeholder photos are waiting on confirmation from the academy before the full launch.
