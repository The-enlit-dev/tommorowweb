## The brief

VibeHeal is a planned AI assistant for “vibe coders” — people who build apps, websites and software with AI tools. It isn’t released yet, so the website had a delicate job: explain the idea clearly, collect early-access sign-ups, and never promise more than exists.

## What we built

- A focused landing page built around one action: joining the waitlist.
- A “status at a glance” panel that labels every capability honestly — *in development*, *planned* or *exploring*.
- Sections for the problem, capabilities, how it’s meant to work, an FAQ and an about page.
- A blog with an RSS feed, and account sign-up and sign-in powered by Clerk.

## Why it matters

Early-stage products live or die on trust. Putting the real status of each feature on the page — with the date it was last updated — makes the pitch more believable, not less.
